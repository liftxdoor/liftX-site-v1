Commercial Case Study • Garden City, Idaho

Three Full-View doors for OutWest Studios.

LIFTX installed three Amarr 3552 black-anodized aluminum-and-glass doors as an older office building was converted into photography and branding studios.

Discuss a Commercial ProjectCommercial Services

Project Overview

Three openings. Three distinct configurations.

The Garden City project called for three commercial Full-View doors in different sizes. LIFTX matched each opening with its confirmed track or operator configuration while keeping the black-anodized frames and tempered-glass specification consistent across the project.

Application

Photography and branding studios in a converted office building.

Door system

Three Amarr 3552 aluminum-and-glass commercial doors.

Finish

Black-anodized aluminum frames.

Glazing

⅛-inch tempered glass.

Commercial Adaptive Reuse

Overhead doors built into a new studio use.

The project transformed an older office building into photography and branding studios. The three Full-View systems became part of that conversion, combining commercial overhead-door operation with a consistent black aluminum-and-glass appearance.

LIFTX coordinated the door sizes, the high-lift opening, and the powered hoist opening as separate systems within one project.
