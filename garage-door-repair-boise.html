Door Balance & Opener Strain

Should a garage door feel heavy when disconnected from the opener?

No. With the opener disconnected, a properly balanced door should lift by hand without excessive force and stay near halfway open. If it feels very heavy, drops, or rises on its own, stop using it and have the spring balance inspected.

Top-section or opener bracket movement

If the opener bracket, reinforcement, or backing begins pulling away from the top section, stop operating the door. The door may be too heavy, out of balance, improperly reinforced, or experiencing more than one problem.

Opener strain

Grinding, slowing, stopping, reversing, rail movement, or a trolley that struggles to move the door can indicate a balance problem rather than an opener problem alone.

Manual balance behavior

When the system is safe to test, the door should move without excessive force and should not rapidly fall or shoot upward. A door that will not remain reasonably controlled needs inspection.

What LIFTX checks

Spring sizing and tension, cables, drums, bearings, track and roller friction, section condition, reinforcement, opener attachment, travel, and force settings are evaluated together.

Do not disconnect an unsafe door: If a spring appears broken, cables are loose, the door is crooked, or the door is stuck, leave it in place and call for service.

Does LIFTX provide garage door repair in Boise and the Treasure Valley?

Yes. LIFTX repairs residential and commercial garage door systems throughout Boise, Garden City, Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Caldwell, Kuna, Star, and Middleton. Extended service is also available for Mountain Home and Valley County when the job scope and schedule support travel.

Can LIFTX provide same-day garage door repair?

Same-day garage door repair is often possible, depending on the schedule, location, door condition, and required parts. Call or text LIFTX with the address, symptoms, and photos so the visit can be prepared accurately.

How can I tell if my garage door spring is broken?

Common signs include a visible gap in a torsion spring, a loud bang from the garage, a door that will not lift, or a door that suddenly feels much heavier than normal.

Can I open my garage door with a broken spring?

No. The spring provides the counterbalance that makes the door manageable. Using the opener or forcing the door can create additional damage and a safety hazard.

Should both garage door springs be replaced?

Yes. On a two-spring system, LIFTX replaces both springs as a matched pair. They have completed the same number of cycles, and replacing only the broken spring leaves the other spring near the end of the same service life.

Does LIFTX repair cables, off-track doors, and garage door openers?

Yes. LIFTX repairs cables, drums, rollers, tracks, hinges, sections, spring systems, and garage door openers. The complete door is inspected first because more than one condition can contribute to the failure.
